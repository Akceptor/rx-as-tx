Connection

Off: only keepalive pings, no RC frames, so the receiver goes to failsafe. Safe (default on connect): sticks centered, throttle and AUX low (disarmed). Live: the gamepad drives the channels. You can only switch to Live with throttle low and ch5 (arm) low. Switches back to Off if the gamepad disconnects, or if the tab is hidden and gamepad data stops updating for 500 ms.
